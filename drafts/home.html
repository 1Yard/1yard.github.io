Why trust 1Yard

We exist to extend care, not just complete tasks.

Our volunteers step in as an extension of our clients' capacity when aging, illness, or mobility makes everyday tasks difficult.

Dignity first

We treat clients as partners and decision-makers when they work with our volunteers.

Safety first

Volunteers always work in pairs and are trained in task safety before their first job.

No financial barriers

Free or pay-what-you-can, with no proof of income required. No one is turned away.

Local

Students from Kingston's high schools and universities, helping their neighbours.

How 1Yard works

Getting help is simple

  1. Reach out

    Request help on our website, or give us a call.

  2. Tell us what you need

    We'll ask about your address, the work, and a few days that suit you.

  3. We find two volunteers

    They pick a time on one of your days, and we let you know.

  4. We show up and do the work

    That's it!

Services are free or pay-what-you-can. No one is turned away due to inability to pay.
